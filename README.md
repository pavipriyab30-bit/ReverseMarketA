# BESTORA AI 🚀

### AI-Powered Reverse Marketplace

**BESTORA AI (ReverseMarketAI)** is an intelligent decision-making platform that transforms the traditional marketplace experience.

Instead of making users search through hundreds of products or services and decide on their own, BESTORA AI understands **what the user actually needs**, searches available options, compares them, evaluates their suitability, identifies potential risks, and recommends the **best option for that specific user**.

### 💡 What BESTORA AI Does

- 🧠 **Understands** user requirements and preferences
- 🔎 **Searches** relevant products and services
- ⚖️ **Compares** available options
- 📊 **Evaluates** price, features, quality, and suitability
- ⚠️ **Warns** users about potential risks and unsuitable choices
- 🤝 **Negotiates** for better-value options where applicable
- 🎯 **Recommends** the most suitable choice instead of simply showing a list

### 🔄 Reverse Marketplace Concept

Traditional marketplaces ask:

**"What do you want to buy?"**

BESTORA AI asks:

**"What problem are you trying to solve?"**

The AI then works backwards from the user's requirements to find the most suitable products or services.

### 🎯 Vision

To build an AI decision engine that makes marketplaces **personalized, intelligent, and user-centric**, helping people make better purchasing and service decisions with less effort.

> **Don't search endlessly. Tell BESTORA what you need — let AI find the best option.**
